Yoga

Finn forma som passar deg

Ashtanga Yoga

Ashtanga yoga is a dynamic form of yoga where you follow a fixed series of yoga poses that build strength, flexibility, and create warmth in the body.

Vinyasa flow

Vinyasa Flow is also dynamic and has its roots in Ashtanga Yoga, but the classes can vary more in structure.

Gentle flow

In gentle yoga we start with simple movements and focus on breath and relaxation.

Yoga for children

In yoga for children, song, rhythm and play are an important part of the session.

Praktisk informasjon

Ta gjerne med eiga treningsmatte om du har, viss ikkje har eg litt matter til utlån. Alle timane avsluttar med ca ti minutt avspenning, så det kan vere godt å ha med eit teppe.
